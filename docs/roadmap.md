# Milestone roadmap

M00 is complete. The user manually tested and approved M01 movement in the M02A brief. M02A is the dedicated visual-development gate: one chamber, original art pipeline and WASD/arrow input equivalence. User visual approval remains pending. M02B has not started and requires its own approved scope after that review. Historical M01 limitations remain recorded in development-log.md, superseded by the user's approval where applicable.

Stop at each gate to measure and revise. Favor one validated representative mechanic over bulk content. A milestone is not complete merely because its source files exist; record failures and unavailable tooling separately from passes. Deferred/rejected ideas belong in design notes, not incidental scope expansion.

Stop after 02A for user visual approval of machinery scale, palette, platform/player readability, atmosphere, diagnostic typography and background depth. No artistic approval is inferred from screenshots or passing tests. Preserve the approved movement and existing camera in later work; region/streaming/checkpoint decisions remain future scope.
