# Rebecca

The approved design uses mint twin-tails, a navy jacket, a narrow stance, and plated blue and pink cybernetic fists. The 160×80 sheet uses 40×40 cells with a (20, 38) foot anchor. It contains four idle keys and static neutral-left, confident, and annoyed poses. Neutral right is the first idle key. Rebecca remains an asset prototype outside the playable scenes.

The idle holds each frame for 375, 125, 250, and 125 ms in order. A one-pixel rise leads the sleeves and the far twin-tail settles on the return. The soles stay planted.
